CCTypewriter is a Sinhala typeface by Damith Welikala (Ceylon Calligraphy), made in 2019. Its letters are drawn with a single even, round-ended stroke, like the letters of a Sinhala typewriter, with simple open vowel signs and a small p-shaped al-lakuna. It suits headings, posters, packaging and short texts that want the plain, mechanical feel of typewritten Sinhala.

The font was first made in the FM Abhaya ASCII layout, the encoding most Sinhala fonts used before Unicode: Sinhala letters were drawn on Latin keys, so text only looked like Sinhala in that one font. In 2026 Mooniak ported it to Unicode. Every glyph now sits on its Sinhala code point or is reached through OpenType rules, the vowel signs that were typed as separate keys are composed, and the signs are positioned exactly where the original drew them.

The font covers the Sinhala letters in common use with their vowel signs, rakaransaya and yansaya, ඦ and ෳ, a set of consonant + vowel-sign ligatures, the conjuncts ක්‍ෂ, ත්‍ව, න්‍ද and ද්‍ර, plus digits and basic punctuation, in a single Regular weight.

To contribute, see github.com/mooniak/cctypewriter-font.
